Keep the flame even.
The Cedar Candle lasts longer when the first burn is patient.
 
01Trim the wick
Keep it near a quarter inch, so the flame stays low and even.
02Let the first burn finish the surface
Burn until the melt pool reaches the glass. That keeps the walls from tunneling.
03Stop before three hours
Blow it out, let the wax set, and trim again before the next light.
