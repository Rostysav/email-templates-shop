# Daybook

A one-year planner for iPad, laid out as an open notebook. Open `index.html` in Safari. Plans, stickers, the theme, and the cover stay on that iPad.

## On iPad

1. AirDrop `index.html` to the iPad, or copy the folder into Files.
2. Open the file in Safari.
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, if you want it beside your apps.
4. On the cover, turn the pages with the arrows and pick a theme underneath, then tap Open.

Writing with a finger or the keyboard works in the plan list. Stickers drag on the day page. Apple Pencil handwriting belongs in GoodNotes; this file keeps typed plans and placed stickers.

## Where your writing is kept

Plans, stickers, goals, notes, the theme, the cover, and the year are saved in the browser on the iPad where you opened the file. There is no account and no server. Nothing is written back into `index.html`.

A reload, a hard reload, closing the tab, or restarting the iPad leaves that writing in place.

It can disappear, and the only copy is the one you export:

- You clear Safari history and website data, or you remove data for this page.
- You use a Private tab. That copy lasts until the private window closes.
- You leave the planner unopened for about a week. Safari may delete stored page data it has not seen you use.
- The iPad is low on free space. iOS then throws out website data.
- You open the file from a different place. The file in Files, a copy served from a computer, and the same file on another port are three separate stores. The writing looks gone because you are looking at an empty one.
- The Home Screen icon and a normal Safari tab on iPad can keep separate stores. Export before you switch between them.

## How to keep a copy

The Folio ribbon, on the right edge of the book, opens theme, year, Export, and Import.

1. Tap **Folio**, then **Export**.
2. Safari downloads `daybook-backup.json`. Save it in Files or iCloud Drive. That file is the copy that survives a cleared browser.
3. If the book opens empty, tap **Folio**, then **Import**, and choose that file. The plans, stickers, goals, notes, theme, cover, and year come back.

Export again after a week of writing, and before you clear Safari, update iOS, or move the HTML file. The planner does not keep a second copy for you.

## What is inside

- Cover, with three themes (Paper, Ink, Tide) and three covers (Index, Mark, Band)
- Year, month, week, and day pages for 2026, 2027, and 2028
- A plan list on each day
- Stickers you can place and drag on the day
- A goals page and four note pages
