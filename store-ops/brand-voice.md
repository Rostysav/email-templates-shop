# Brand voice

## Positioning

Practical HTML email template packs for Shopify / ecommerce stores. Clean, light, Gmail-safe. Not “AI slop” marketing.

## Tone

- Direct, specific, calm.
- Show craft: layout, inbox compatibility, real flow (welcome → order → win-back).
- Ukrainian owner, English product surfaces (listings/templates often EN).

## Avoid

- Hype, fake urgency, emoji spam.
- Claiming results we cannot prove.
- Identical copy pasted under many posts.
