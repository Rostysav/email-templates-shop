# Platform rules

## Gumroad

- Source of truth for products and pricing.
- Fix listing quality before new promo pushes.
- Ukraine payouts may be blocked — do not waste runs on payout settings unless asked.

## LinkedIn

- Professional tone: ecommerce ops, email flows, Shopify lifecycle.
- Reply to comments on our posts.
- Comment on others: insights, not hard sells. Link in profile / Featured, not every comment.

## X (Twitter)

- Shorter, sharper. Engage niche accounts (email, Shopify, indie makers).
- Pin / keep Gumroad discovery via profile; avoid reply-spam with links.

## Reddit

- Highest risk of removal for promo.
- Default: helpful comments only, no Gumroad URL.
- If a thread explicitly asks for template resources and rules allow, soft mention + disclosure.
- Never cross-post the same promo text across subs.
- Prefer subs: ecommerce tooling, Shopify, email marketing — respect each sub’s rules.

## General

- Check notifications / replies before new outbound comments.
- Rotate niches; do not hit the same 5 posts every run.
- Cap outbound comments per run (~5–10 total across platforms) unless engagement is clearly welcome.
