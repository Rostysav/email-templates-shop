AFTER THE BOX
A little care.
Wipe the brass with a dry cloth. Keep the ink bottles upright and away from direct sun. That is enough.
THE LAMP
Dust the shade from the outside. Do not soak the brass base.
THE INK
Cap each bottle after use. A small shake before writing is enough.
